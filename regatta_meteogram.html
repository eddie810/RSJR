02How the flag is set

GREEN — ≤ 20 km/h
Light to moderate wind. Chop at the far buoys stays small (a few cm). Fair, rowable water.
YELLOW — 20–30 km/h
Building wind. A down-the-course westerly makes the return leg into a headwind and stacks chop toward the buoys — watch closely.
RED — > 30 km/h sustained
Committee's hold threshold. Rough, unfair water and safety risk for low-freeboard shells.
Local adjustments applied South winds are shadowed here. The lake sits in the lee for due-south winds, and the models over-forecast them — so only winds from the south sector (SSE–SSW) are discounted (about −20 % to −40 %, strongest from due south); winds from any other direction stand as forecast. Down-the-course westerlies are flagged with a ⚠ even when the speed itself is green, because they build the worst chop at the turn buoys and turn the home leg into a headwind. The flag reads the ECCC RDPS model wind; The Weather Company supplies the sky, temperature and shower chance.
